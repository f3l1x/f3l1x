## dayjs

```php
return dayjs.duration(time, unit)
    .format('Y [years] M [months] D [days] H [hours] m [minutes] s [seconds]')
    .replace(/\b0 years\b/, '')
    .replace(/\b0 months\b/, '')
    .replace(/\b0 days\b/, '')
    .replace(/\b0 hours\b/, '')
    .replace(/\b0 minutes\b/, '')
    .replace(/\b0 seconds\b/, '')
    .trim();
```
