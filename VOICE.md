# Voice

A developer talking to another developer: plain, practical, a little dry. No pitch, no performance.

## Rules

- Say the useful thing first; background after.
- Plain words, short sentences.
- Concrete over impressive: name the tool, the version, the command. Show the snippet, explain it in a line or two.
- Honest, a bit self-deprecating. "I don't know yet" is fine.
- Warm, not salesy. No hype, no superlatives.
- Facts about the work, not adjectives about the person.

## Language

- Czech for posts aimed at the Czech/Slovak community; English for everything else (wiki, notes, descriptions, UI).
- One language per page. Do not translate just to have both.
- Keep technical terms in their original form.

## Formatting

- Titles descriptive and specific (`Topic: specific thing`). No clickbait.
- ISO dates (`2025-01-09`), plain digits.
- Commands in fenced blocks with a language; inline `code` for names, flags and paths.
- Link to primary sources; link text says what it is.
- Lists for parallel items, prose for arguments.
- Emoji rare, never in titles, headings or descriptions.

## Descriptions

Factual, 90 to 155 characters, one or two sentences, no URL, no ellipsis, no hype. Say what is on the page.

> Talk by Milan Šulc on the Command Bus pattern, given at moderntv.eu in April 2024. Slides and the GitHub repository are linked.

## AI-assisted writing

- Match this voice in the page's own language.
- Never invent facts, quotes, numbers or what was said in a talk.
- No filler intros, no summaries repeating the page, no AI attribution.
